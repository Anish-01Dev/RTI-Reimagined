# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors are expected to engage respectfully and in good faith. Disagreement over technical direction is expected and welcome; personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language are not tolerated.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other communication channel used for this project.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this standard and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with it.
