# RTI Legal Glossary

Reference terms from the Right to Information Act, 2005, as used throughout this repository. This is a working glossary for engineering and product purposes, not legal advice.

**RTI** — Right to Information. The statutory mechanism through which a citizen may request information from a public authority.

**PIO / CPIO / SPIO** — Public Information Officer. CPIO refers to a Central Public Information Officer; SPIO refers to a State Public Information Officer. These officers are responsible for processing RTI requests within their authority.

**Nodal Officer** — A routing and coordination role within the Central RTI Online system. Applications submitted through the online portal reach the Nodal Officer first, who transmits them to the concerned CPIO.

**Section 6** — Governs how an RTI request is made.

**Section 6(3)** — Governs transfer of an application where another public authority holds the requested information or is more closely connected to its subject matter. The Act sets a maximum transfer period of five days.

**Section 7** — Governs disposal of a request, including the standard 30-day response period and the 48-hour period applicable to requests concerning the life or liberty of a person.

**Section 8** — Sets out exemptions from disclosure, including personal information under Section 8(1)(j).

**Section 19** — Establishes the appeal mechanism, including the First Appeal route following an adverse decision or a failure to respond within the applicable period.

**Section 20** — Provides for a penalty in specified circumstances, subject to statutory limits, determined through the Information Commission's own process.

**CIC / SIC** — Central Information Commission / State Information Commission.

**First Appeal** — An appeal against an adverse decision, or against a failure to respond within the applicable statutory period, made to the relevant appellate authority within the same public authority.

**Second Appeal** — A further appeal to the relevant Information Commission following an unfavorable or absent outcome at the First Appeal stage.
